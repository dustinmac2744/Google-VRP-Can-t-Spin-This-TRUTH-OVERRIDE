### Independent Confirmation of Infrastructure‑Level AI Exploits (May 11, 2026)

Multiple independent news outlets — including AP, NBC, Politico, Bloomberg, CNBC, and BroadbandBreakfast — all reported on May 11, 2026 that Google disrupted a real‑world attack where:

* Hackers used an AI model to discover and weaponize a previously unknown vulnerability
* The exploit bypassed authentication controls (including 2FA)
* The vulnerability existed inside a widely‑used system administration tool
* The attackers built a working zero‑day exploit using AI
* A mass‑exploitation event was already being prepared

The BroadbandBreakfast report specifically confirms that attackers used AI to exploit an unknown weakness in a company’s digital defense, demonstrating that the threat was active, operational, and infrastructure‑level — not theoretical.
